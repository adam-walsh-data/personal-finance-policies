# Terms of Use

**Last updated: 20 November 2026**

## 1. About the project

Personal Finance Aggregator is a personal project operated by Adam Walsh to view and analyse his own financial-account information. It is not offered as a public service to customers.

## 2. Development status

The backend infrastructure and a server-side integration endpoint have been created. Bank API credentials, bank authorisation sessions and automated synchronisation have not yet been activated. No live bank-data aggregation is currently being performed. Features, supported institutions and availability may change during development.

## 3. Intended use

The intended use is personal financial organisation and analysis by the account holder. You must not use the project to access accounts or data without authorisation, or to violate any law or another person's rights.

The integration is designed for read-only account-information permissions. It is not designed to make payments, transfer funds, place orders, or alter information held by a bank.

Information displayed by the project, when available, is provided for personal information and organisational purposes only. It is not financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice, and is not a recommendation to make any financial decision. You remain responsible for checking information against your bank's own records and obtaining qualified advice where appropriate.

The project is provided in development. No guarantee is made that information will be complete, current, accurate, available at any particular time, or synchronised without delay or error. A bank or account-information provider may restrict, change or discontinue access.

Do not rely on the project as the sole record of account balances, transactions, payment obligations or financial position. The relevant bank's own records remain the authoritative source for its accounts.

## 7. Security and access

The project is designed for private use and read-only account-information access. Do not share access tokens, credentials, the Enable Banking private key, or financial information publicly. Bank API credentials have not yet been configured in the server-side environment. No claim is made that the developing system has undergone an independent security audit.
