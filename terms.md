# Terms of Use

**Last updated: 20 November 2026**

## 1. About the project

Personal Finance Aggregator is a personal project being developed to help its operator view and analyse their own financial-account information in one place. It is not currently an operational banking-data service and is not offered as a public service to customers.

## 2. Development status

Bank connections and automated synchronisation are not yet active. No bank-data aggregation functionality is currently being offered through this project. Features, supported institutions and availability may change during development.

## 3. Intended use

The intended use is personal financial organisation and analysis by the account holder. You must not use the project to access accounts or data without authorisation, or to violate any law or another person's rights.

The project is intended to use read-only account-information permissions. It is not intended to make payments, transfer funds, place orders, or alter information held by a bank.

## 4. No financial, tax or legal advice

Information displayed by the project, when available, is provided for personal information and organisational purposes only. It is not financial, investment, tax, accounting or legal advice, and is not a recommendation to make any financial decision. You remain responsible for checking information against your bank's own records and obtaining qualified advice where appropriate.

## 5. Accuracy and availability

The project is provided in development. No guarantee is made that information will be complete, current, accurate, available at any particular time, or synchronised without delay or error. A bank or account-information provider may restrict, change or discontinue access.

Do not rely on the project as the sole record of your account balances, transactions, payment obligations or financial position. Your bank's records remain the authoritative source for your accounts.

## 7. Security and access

The project is being designed for private use and read-only access. Do not share access tokens, credentials, exported financial information or other sensitive account information publicly. Security measures will be documented more fully before live bank-data processing begins.
