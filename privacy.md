## 3. Intended use and data

If and when the application is activated, it is intended for the account holder's personal use to bring together information from financial accounts they choose to connect.

Depending on the institution and the permissions granted, the application may process:
- account names, identifiers, account type and currency;
- balances and balance timestamps;
- transaction amounts, currencies, descriptions, booking and value dates, and available counterparty or merchant details; and
- technical information needed to connect accounts, refresh data, prevent duplicate imports and diagnose sync errors.

The application is being designed for read-only account-information access. It is not intended to initiate payments, transfer funds, or change account settings.

## 4. Purposes

The intended purposes are to display a consolidated view of the account holder's own finances, review spending, reconcile transactions, and support personal budgeting and analysis. The data is not intended for advertising, sale, profiling of other people, or use by other users.

## 5. Financial institutions and service providers

The project is being designed to use Enable Banking for account-information connectivity, subject to the permissions and availability of the relevant financial institution. Enable Banking and each connected bank or payment institution may process information under their own terms and privacy notices.

The final hosting, database, backup and operational service providers have not yet been selected or activated. This notice will be updated with the relevant providers and their roles before live bank data is processed.

## 6. Sharing

The project does not intend to sell personal or financial information or share it for advertising. When operational, information may be processed by the account-information provider and technical providers strictly as needed to operate the application. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

## 7. Storage, security and retention

No bank data is currently being stored by this application. The final storage location, retention period, backup arrangements and deletion process have not yet been implemented or verified. This notice will be updated with those details before live processing begins.

Planned safeguards include limiting access to the account holder, using read-only permissions, protecting credentials and tokens separately from transaction records, and encrypting data in transit and at rest. These are design goals, not a claim that a live system has already implemented or tested them.

## 8. Your choices and data protection rights

The account holder will be able to stop future account-information access by revoking the relevant consent or access through the provider or financial institution, where supported. The application will provide a way to delete locally stored data once that functionality is implemented.

Depending on the applicable law and the circumstances, data protection rights may include access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability. Privacy questions can be sent to the contact address above.

## 9. Changes

This notice will be revised before the application begins processing live bank data and when material changes are made. The date at the top indicates the latest revision.
